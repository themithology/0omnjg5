## 🚀 How to use

1. Open **https://theogpiratebot.vercel.app** in any browser (best experience: **Brave** for the cleanest in-player experience).
2. Search a title or browse Trending / Popular / Anime / Schedule.
3. Tap **Play** — the player lets you switch streaming servers anytime.
4. Tap **Download** on any episode or movie:
   - **Anime** → choose **Sub** or **Dub** in the centered selector → the download portal opens in a new tab.
   - **Movies/Series** → the multi-server download portal opens with quality and language options.
5. Optional: bookmark titles with **My List**, resume from **Continue Watching**, or install the app via your browser's "Add to Home Screen".

> ⌨️ Keyboard QoL on desktop: press **`/`** anywhere to open Search · **`←` / `→`** previous & next episode in the player · **S / D** pick Sub or Dub on the download screen · **2×click** the video for fullscreen.

## ❓ FAQ

**Is TheOGPirateBot free?**
Yes — completely free. No account, no subscription, no credit card.

**Do I need to sign up?**
No. There is no registration of any kind. Your Continue Watching and My List live only in your own browser.

**Can I watch anime subbed and dubbed?**
Yes. Anime titles offer separate Sub and Dub servers across three HD tiers (HD-1, HD-2, HD-3).

**Can I download episodes and movies?**
Yes. Tap Download on any episode or title. For anime you first pick Sub or Dub, then ZokoAnime's download portal opens in a new tab. Other titles open a multi-server download portal with different qualities and languages.

**Is there an app?**
TheOGPirateBot is an installable web app (PWA). On mobile or desktop, open the site and choose "Add to Home Screen" / "Install" — it behaves exactly like a native app.

**Does TheOGPirateBot host videos on its own servers?**
No. TheOGPirateBot is an indexing and discovery web app; it embeds video players and download portals served by third-party hosts. It does not store or upload media files.

**Why is a server missing for my episode?**
Servers are probed continuously. If a host can't serve an episode, its button is hidden automatically — switch to another server in the player.
